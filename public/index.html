How It Works · कैसे काम करता है

1️⃣ Report

Citizens submit problems with photos, videos and location in Hindi or English.

2️⃣ Expert Review

Domain experts score and triage each submission using a structured rubric.

3️⃣ Adopt & Solve

HEIs and industry partners adopt problems, form student teams and prototype.

4️⃣ Deploy & Impact

Solutions are piloted, deployed and tracked to measurable community impact.

Who Participates · कौन जुड़ता है

👨‍👩‍👧 Citizens & Communities

Report and endorse problems, track resolution, and co-create solutions for your own village, block and district.

🎓 Higher Education Institutions

Faculty and students get authentic, credit-linked (NEP 2020) project experience by solving real community problems.

🏭 Industry & Startups

Co-fund solutions through CSR, sponsor pilots, and collaborate with universities on deployment and scale-up.
